# mc-launcher-legacy
This repo is a public archive for those third-party Minecraft Launcher made by Chinese authors.

此仓库是国产《我的世界》国际服第三方启动器的存档仓库。

## Why this repo exists? 为什么会这样
On an *ordinary* afternoon of October 20, 2026, alarming news emerged in China. A Chinese-style parent has reported a third-party commercial Minecraft multiplayer server, along with PCL2, over in-app purchases made by a minor that the parent deemed unaffordable.
One might argue that the third-party server or the minor is to blame, but what has sadly happened is that ALL THE LAUNCHERS HAVE STOPPED PROVIDING DOWNLOAD SERVICES FOR *LEGAL REASONS*. And that's why this repo exists.

在2026年10月20日下午，一位中国家长举报了《我的世界》一个第三方商业服务器以及启动器 PCL II，因为Ta未成年的孩子在这个商业服务器中充了太多钱。
尽管该服务器和孩子谁该负责尚未定论，不幸的是所有这些启动器都遭到波及，因为一些原因暂停了《我的世界》下载服务以及社区资源下载服务。

We have collected all the official legacy versions, whose download services are still available.
Note: **If you download them, run them offline and disable auto-update immediately,** or them will be updated to a newer version and prevent you from the download serivces. 

本仓库仅用于记录这个历史，不具备其他功能。

## How files are obtained? 事件的经过
All files were obtained from the official distribution site, before the authors withdrew them, at a time when their download services were still available

> **Additional Note:**
> Some these archives are INFECTED by people with ulterior motives. By running the infected binaries, your computer would be exposed to great threat.
> Also, there are other people charge of the sources, though they did before the incident. However, whatever they wanna, all these launchers are completely FREE to download.
> Last, due to unknown OTA or other factors, the download services might be disabled in the archived versions.

小编还在收集中，待理清后立马更新……

> **辟谣：**
> 基于已有信息，以下可以确认：
> * 网易《我的世界》和库洛《鸣潮》与本次事件没有任何关联，所谓“应用商店搜不到”只是因为没有(停止运营)渠道服，故应用商店不提供下载
